# AionMarket

A compact AION 2 market companion for Windows 11 x64.

Download **AionMarket-Setup-1.2.20.0.exe** from the [latest release](https://github.com/SkyPwner/AionMarket-Releases/releases/latest). Setup installs for the current Windows user.

- Track selected item prices and their age in a quiet in-game widget. Controls appear on hover.
- **Alt+Z** opens the main window; change the shortcut in Settings.
- Browse items by category, rarity and equipment level; sort by price or estimated profit.
- The calculator includes the 2% listing and 10% sale fees. Changing purchase price updates an untouched sale target; the profit target is configurable.
- **Helper** compares buying the first one to four complete lots and reselling below the next offer. It runs in the background and never buys or sells for you.
- Current offers average is quantity-weighted. Below your sale counts observed cheaper lots. Pages may be incomplete or change during a read.
- History and the 24h average describe observed listing prices, not completed sales. Missing history is not invented.
- **Craft** shows supported crafted equipment, upgrade levels, required materials and estimated cost including gold equipment. No inventory/Owned tracking. The optional 25% craft comparison is an assumption, not a guaranteed success rate.
- Connect a public character from Craft to import class and equipped gear only. The last good loadout stays saved across teleports and outages. Refresh is manual or every five minutes; website replication can lag. Class artwork is static, not your character appearance.

## Connection and updates

Launch AION 2 from the companion and open Market once. TShark/Wireshark and Npcap are separate first-connection prerequisites; Setup does not install drivers or launch the game. A valid game session can resume after updating only the companion.

Use Check for updates and apply the update. AionMarket closes its previous instance and starts the new one through a stable launcher path. The signed feed and package hash are verified before activation.

## Support

Settings contains Open logs and Create support report. Do not share private captures or key files. Restricted game verification can require matching the game process permissions.

Market availability, replica delays and scan speed depend on the service and network. Price gaps do not guarantee a sale or profit. No shared cloud history is included.
