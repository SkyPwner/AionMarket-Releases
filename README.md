# AionMarket

A compact AION 2 market companion for Windows 11 x64.

Download [AionMarket 1.2.26 installer](https://github.com/SkyPwner/AionMarket-Releases/releases/download/v1.2.26.0/AionMarket-Setup-1.2.26.0.exe). Setup installs for the current Windows user.

- A quiet game widget shows selected item prices and their age, with controls on hover.
- Watchlist includes search, colored rarity and equipment filters, price/profit sorting and a configurable profit threshold.
- The profit calculator includes the 2% listing fee and 10% sale fee. A purchase-price change updates the automatic sale target; manual sale edits remain yours.
- Current offers show a quantity-weighted average and how many observed offers are cheaper than your sale price.
- Craft shows character equipment, known levels and recipes. Upgrade cost uses the equipped starting item and includes required additional equipment and materials.
- The compact Craft cost panel supports quick checks beside the Watchlist calculator.
- Price history charts saved observations. Recorded listing prices are estimates, not confirmed sales or guaranteed profit.
- Enter a character nickname to load public equipped-item information. Last-known equipment is retained through unavailable refreshes.
- Alt+Z opens the main window; change the shortcut in Settings.
- Updates preserve settings and the pinned launcher, and do not require restarting the game.

Helper is temporarily disabled.

This repository distributes installers, packages and signed update metadata. It does not contain the private development checkout or publisher key.
