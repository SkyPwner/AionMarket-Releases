# AionMarket

A Windows companion for AION 2: a compact in-game price widget, market watchlist, profit calculator, price history and crafting cost planner.

- Track selected items and view current observed market prices.
- Calculate sale prices and estimated profit after listing and sale fees.
- Explore saved price history and 24-hour averages.
- View your character's equipment and estimate upgrades for one item or the full equipped set.
- Keep the widget compact while playing. Helper is temporarily disabled.
